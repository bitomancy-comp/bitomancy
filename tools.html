How to use these tools

PDF and image tools

Add files, reorder them with the arrows, then click the button. Image to PDF can fit each page to the image or place it on A4. Use Split to keep only the pages you need, like 1-3, 5.

Calculators

Enter your numbers and click Calculate. EMI, SIP and GST work in rupees. The position size tool shows how many units keep a stop-loss within your chosen risk. Trading tools are educational, not advice. Looking for signals? See Bitomancy Scalper.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. No account is needed.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. PDF, image and text tools run in your browser, so files never leave your device.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large PDFs depend on your device memory and can be slow on phones.

Why does a converted file look different?

JPG is lossy and flattens transparency to white. Use PNG for transparency or exact pixels.

Are the calculators financial advice?

No. They are estimates based on what you enter. Real rates, fees and taxes differ.
