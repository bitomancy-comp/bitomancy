Free AI Plans in India: What Still Works (Nov 2026)

By Suraj Sharma, Founder of BITOMANCY

The free-AI giveaway season in India is over. Here is what is still open, what is about to start costing money, and a calculator to find your own renewal date.

Between late 2025 and early 2026, India became the one place where premium AI subscriptions were handed out almost like data packs. Reliance Jio bundled Google's AI Pro plan with its 5G recharges, OpenAI gave away a year of ChatGPT Go, and Airtel bundled Perplexity Pro for its customers. Millions of people claimed one.

Now the calendar has caught up. Jio's window shut on 29 October 2026, the other two closed earlier in the year, and the first free ChatGPT Go years are rolling over into paid plans this month. This guide covers what is still available, what to check on your own account, and how to avoid paying for something you did not mean to keep. We last checked public offer terms and news reports on 10 November 2026.

Quick answer: which free AI offers are still open?

For new users, none of the three big telecom and OpenAI giveaways is open any more. If you already claimed one, the benefit continues until your own end date.

If you claimed ChatGPT Go: decide before the renewal date

OpenAI's free year required a payment method on file, which means the first charge can arrive on its own once the twelve months are up. Anyone who claimed in the first days of November 2025 is reaching that point now. A simple routine avoids a surprise:

  • Find the date. Open your plan or subscription settings in ChatGPT and note the renewal date. The calculator above gives a cross-check.
  • Check the price shown. The renewal is charged at the plan price in force at that time, not at any earlier number you may remember. In October 2026 checks, ChatGPT Go was listed at ₹399 a month.
  • Decide honestly. If you opened ChatGPT fewer than a few times a week this year, the free tier is likely enough. If you hit message or image limits regularly, ₹399 may be fair value.
  • Cancel early if you are unsure. Cancel at least a few days before the date. If you set up UPI AutoPay, also review the mandate inside your UPI app so nothing is left active.

If you claimed Jio's Google AI Pro: protect your eligibility

Jio's offer ties the benefit to your mobile plan, not just your Google account. The terms require an eligible unlimited 5G plan (₹349 or more, with a limited-period 1.5 GB/day option from ₹299) to stay active, and they exclude corporate or enterprise SIMs. Let your recharge lapse or switch to a cheaper plan and access can be suspended while your 18-month clock keeps running. Porting your number out of Jio cancels it.

So the practical rule is simple: before you change plans or switch operators, check whether you are giving up months of a plan that would otherwise cost ₹1,950 a month. Also read what Google's own subscription page says about what happens at the end of the 18 months, rather than assuming either way.

Be careful with "claim after the deadline" tricks

Every time a popular offer ends, a wave of pages appears promising a "secret link", a "working bypass" or a heavily discounted ready-made account. We would stay away from all of them. Offers like these are tied to your own mobile number and Google account, so anything sold or shared by a stranger is likely to be a shared login, a revoked subscription or a way to harvest your details. Never give out OTPs or your Google password, and if a deal is only valid through someone else's account, treat that as the warning sign it is.

If your free period ends and you still want a paid plan, rupee-billed plans are usually the cheapest route, since they avoid foreign-card fees. These are the list prices reported in October 2026 checks of Indian checkout pages and app stores.

You may not need to pay at all

The major assistants all keep a free tier, with lower limits and fewer premium features. For writing emails, summarising documents, learning a topic or debugging a small script, that is often plenty. A practical test: pay only if you keep hitting a free limit at least once a week. If you only hit it now and then, wait for the reset or switch tools for that task.

Using these plans for work or client projects

All three giveaways were consumer plans linked to an individual's account or phone number. That is fine for personal learning, but be cautious with client material. Consumer plans typically do not include the data-processing terms or admin controls that business plans offer, and they may not produce an invoice in your business name. A sensible pattern for freelancers and small teams is to use free or low-cost plans to find which assistant suits your work, then move to the vendor's business tier for anything involving customer data. Keep your own records tidy too, including GST-ready invoices for your clients.

Key Takeaways

  • New claims are closed for Jio's Google AI Pro, ChatGPT Go's free year and Airtel's Perplexity Pro.
  • If you already claimed, your benefit runs from your own activation date, not a fixed calendar date.
  • ChatGPT Go free years are converting to paid plans now, so check your renewal date and cancel early if you do not want it.
  • Jio's Google AI Pro depends on keeping a qualifying plan active, so think before changing recharge or operator.
  • Ignore "after-deadline" links and cheap resold accounts, because the offers are tied to your own number and login.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChatGPT Go still free in India?

Not for new users. OpenAI's India promotion for a free year of ChatGPT Go began in November 2025 and, according to its release notes, ended on 21 January 2026. People who claimed during that window keep their free year, after which the saved payment method is charged the current monthly price.

Can I still get Jio's free Google AI Pro offer?

Jio's terms set the offer period as 30 October 2025 to 29 October 2026, so new claims have closed unless Jio announces otherwise. If you claimed in time, you keep the 18 months from your activation date as long as you maintain an eligible plan.

Is Perplexity Pro still free with Airtel?

No new sign-ups. Indian tech coverage reports that Airtel's free Perplexity Pro offer closed on 16 January 2026. If you activated it earlier, you keep 12 months from the day you activated.

What is the cheapest paid AI plan in India?

Based on October 2026 list prices, ChatGPT Go and Google AI Plus are both ₹399 a month, which makes them the lowest-cost paid options from the two biggest consumer assistants. Prices and promotions change, so confirm at checkout.

Conclusion

The free-AI rush gave millions of Indians a real chance to try premium tools, but free periods always end, and the quiet part is the renewal. Spend five minutes this week to find your end date, decide whether the tool earned its price, and cancel or keep it on purpose. If you do want to keep paying, ₹399 plans cover most people, and the free tiers cover more than you might expect.
