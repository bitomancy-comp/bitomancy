InvoiceKit: Offline GST Invoice Generator for India

Create clean, GST-ready invoices without a monthly subscription. Unzip it, open it in your browser and start billing. No install, no account, no internet needed.

Get InvoiceKit → One-time purchase · secure checkout on Gumroad

GST and tax

  • CGST + SGST, IGST or no tax: InvoiceKit can pick the right split automatically from your GSTIN and the customer's GSTIN, or from the place of supply (state) you choose, which helps when the customer has no GSTIN.
  • Reverse charge (RCM) option: when GST is payable by the recipient, switch on RCM. The GST is calculated and shown for the recipient but not added to your invoice total, and the bill prints "Reverse charge: YES/NO".
  • All GST rates, including 40%: 0, 0.1, 0.25, 1.5, 3, 5, 12, 18, 28 and 40%, plus a custom rate. Under GST 2.0 (effective 22 September 2025) the main slabs are 5% and 18%, with 40% for specified goods. The old 12% and 28% rates are kept for older invoices, credit notes and debit notes. Always check the correct rate for each item.
  • GSTIN check: your GSTIN and the customer's GSTIN are checked for format and check digit as you type.
  • Prices with or without GST: enter rates excluding or including GST, with discounts (percent or flat), shipping or other charges with their own GST, and round-off.

Invoice layout

  • Serial number (S.No) on every item, with HSN/SAC, quantity, unit (Nos, Kg, Ltr, Hrs and more), rate, taxable value, GST % and amount.
  • Authorised signatory box in the bottom-right corner of the bill, with "For your company name", space to sign, an optional signature or stamp image and the signatory's name.
  • Six document types: Tax Invoice, Proforma Invoice, Quotation, Credit Note, Debit Note and Bill of Supply.
  • Your branding: logo, accent colour, company name, GSTIN and address, plus a PO or reference number for each invoice.
  • Amount in words in Indian format (Lakh, Crore, Rupees and Paise), and a tax summary by HSN/SAC.
  • Bank and UPI details and default terms printed on every invoice, with advance paid and balance due.
  • PDF download on A4 using your browser's "Save as PDF". The PDF is named from your invoice number.

Customers, records and backup

  • Saved customers, and a Duplicate invoice button that copies a bill with the next invoice number.
  • Payment tracking (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue) with your outstanding total.
  • CSV export for your accountant, with taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, reverse charge and totals for every saved invoice.
  • Backup and restore of all your data, and ₹, $, €, £ or AED currencies.
  • Large, private storage: invoices are kept in your browser's IndexedDB database, which holds thousands of invoices, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
