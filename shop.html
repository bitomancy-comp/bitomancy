InvoiceKit: GST Invoice Generator

Create clean, GST-ready invoices without a monthly subscription. Unzip it, open it in your browser and start billing. No install, no account, no internet needed.

Get InvoiceKit → One-time purchase · secure checkout on Gumroad

What's included

  • GST tax splits: choose CGST + SGST (same state), IGST (other state) or no tax, with totals calculated for you.
  • Your branding: upload a logo, pick an accent colour and show your company name, GSTIN and address.
  • Item-level GST: HSN/SAC, quantity, rate and GST slabs of 0, 5, 12, 18 and 28%.
  • Discounts and extra charges, plus the amount in words in Indian format (Lakh, Crore, Rupees and Paise).
  • Bank and UPI details and default terms printed on every invoice.
  • PDF download using your browser's "Save as PDF".
  • Saved customers and payment tracking (Paid, Unpaid, Overdue) with your outstanding total.
  • Backup and restore of all your data, and ₹, $, €, £ or AED currencies.

Who it's for

Freelancers, shopkeepers, agencies, consultants and small businesses in India who need quick, professional GST invoices without a complicated accounting tool.

How it works

  1. Click Get InvoiceKit and complete checkout on Gumroad.
  2. Download and unzip the file, then open InvoiceKit.html in your browser.
  3. Add your company details and logo, add a customer and items, then click Download PDF.
  4. To remove the trial notice and 5-invoice limit, email your Machine ID and Gumroad receipt to support.bitomancy@gmail.com and paste the activation code you receive.

Good to know

  • Your data is stored only in your own browser and is never uploaded. Use Download backup regularly.
  • One licence covers one user, one business and one computer (the browser you activate in).
  • InvoiceKit is a billing template, not tax advice. Please check GST rules for your business.

Questions

Is it a subscription? No. It is a one-time purchase; see the Gumroad page for the current price.

Do I need internet? No, InvoiceKit works fully offline.

How do I get support or move to a new computer? Email support.bitomancy@gmail.com with your Gumroad receipt.

Are there refunds? Refund terms are shown on the Gumroad product page at checkout.
